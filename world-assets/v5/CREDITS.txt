Music: cottonwood-waltz.m4a (plays softly under the water when sound is on)
“Cottonwood Waltz”
An original piece made for this site in 2026: a slow waltz in F major for piano,
harp and strings, about 2 minutes 40 seconds, then a short pause before it repeats.
Every note was written for this piece, then played with the single-note
instrument recordings credited below. No melody, score or recording of any
other piece of music was used.

Piano recordings: “Salamander Grand Piano V3” by Alexander Holm,
licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 (CC BY 3.0).
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/
Source: https://archive.org/details/SalamanderGrandPianoV3
MP3 edition used: the @audio-samples/piano-mp3 packages on npm.
Changes: single notes were re-pitched, shaped, mixed with the other
instruments and given a room echo. The recordings are not shared separately.

Harp and string recordings: “Versilian Studios Chamber Orchestra 2, Community Edition”
(VSCO 2 CE) by Versilian Studios, released into the public domain (CC0 1.0).
https://github.com/sgossner/VSCO-2-CE
Editions used: the harp recordings straight from VSCO 2 CE, and the violin, viola
and cello sections and the solo double bass from tiny-orchestra (on npm, from VSCO 2 CE).

Garden drawings
The terraced garden, the hoopoe and the other line drawings: directed by

The watercolor garden objects in the world behind the text are rendered
