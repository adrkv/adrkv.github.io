Planning EV charging around fleet operations

Connecting vehicle readiness, site capacity, and energy cost to a feasible charging schedule.

Automotive & EVCharging operations

An EV charging plan has to meet the vehicle’s next assignment within the power available at the site. Energy prices, other building loads, and local generation affect when charging is attractive. Arrival times, departure times, and battery requirements determine how much flexibility is actually available.

This paper presents a planning framework for managed charging, developed from EV-infrastructure use-case work. It connects business priorities to the data and decisions needed for a pilot. The capabilities described are proposals for evaluation in a focused pilot.
