FROM OPEN BASELINE TO PRIVATE DEPLOYMENT

Start ahead, then shape it on your own branches.

Every PeakWhale™ project ships as an Apache 2.0 foundation you can adopt at zero license cost. Instead of building pipelines, APIs, and safety checks from a blank page, your team starts from a tested baseline and shapes it privately through your own commits.

  1. 01
    Fork the open baseline

    Start with a working CLI, FastAPI service, MCP server, and synthetic test data in minutes instead of weeks.

  2. 02
    Shape on private branches

    Clone into your own private repo. Connect internal tables, edit YAML rules, and commit logic without exposing your data.

  3. 03
    Run solo or co-build

    Deploy on your own hardware at zero software cost, or book 30 minutes to tailor the architecture to your stack.
